PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy.
Handled with care.

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who operates KiteLearn

KiteLearn is an independently developed and operated adult financial-education platform. It is not represented as a registered company or as an institution affiliated with SEBI, a stock exchange or a government body. Privacy enquiries can be sent to hello@kitelearn.in.

Information we collect

When you create an account, KiteLearn processes the name and email address you provide, a securely hashed password (not your plain-text password), email-verification and account-security information, and learning activity such as lessons started or completed and quiz attempts and results. The platform may also process technical request information needed to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

Account information is used to create and secure your account, verify your email, provide password recovery, save learning progress, operate quizzes and certificates of course completion, respond to support requests, prevent abuse and maintain the service. We do not sell personal information.

Technical logs and hosting

KiteLearn is hosted using Cloudflare services. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, device/browser information, request time and requested resource for network delivery, security and reliability under its own service terms and privacy documentation. KiteLearn does not write raw visitor IP addresses, user-agent strings or location into its application visitor logs. For authentication abuse prevention, the server receives the request IP and converts it into a one-way hashed rate-limit key; these hashed rate-limit records are automatically deleted after 30 days. Cloudflare may separately process technical request information under its service terms.

Analytics and optional advertising tools

Google Analytics 4 may be used to understand aggregate website usage and learning engagement. Analytics is optional and should be enabled only after you make a choice in the analytics preference control. Microsoft Clarity is not enabled. Google Ads conversion tracking and Meta Pixel are not enabled unless separately configured and disclosed before activation. KiteLearn does not intentionally send passwords, raw authentication credentials or lesson-response text to analytics providers.

Cookies and browser storage

Optional analytics preference:

Essential browser storage and a session cookie are used to maintain sign-in and platform functionality. The app may use local storage for preferences and learning experience features. Optional analytics storage is controlled separately through the analytics preference control. Clearing browser storage may sign you out or reset locally stored preferences.

Sharing and service providers

Information is shared only where needed to operate the platform, such as Cloudflare hosting/database infrastructure, Resend transactional email delivery and Google Analytics when enabled, or where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users. Service providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion requests

Account and learning records are retained while an account remains active and as reasonably necessary for security, service operation, dispute handling and legal obligations. We do not publish a fixed deletion timeline where one has not been established. You may request access, correction or deletion of your account information by emailing hello@kitelearn.in from your registered email address. Some records may need to be retained where lawfully required or temporarily remain in protected backups.

Your choices and privacy requests

You may choose whether to enable optional analytics, keep your account details accurate, and request access, correction or deletion. Please include enough information for us to identify the account safely; never send your password, OTP, UPI PIN or banking credentials. We will respond to privacy requests through the contact address below.

Adults and eligibility

KiteLearn is designed and marketed as a beginner-friendly learning tool for adults. It is not designed as a child-directed service. Please do not create an account on behalf of a child.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the platform or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Contact and privacy grievances

For privacy questions, access/correction/deletion requests or complaints, email hello@kitelearn.in. Please use the subject “KiteLearn Privacy Request”.
