Building Your Investment Strategy

2 mins readLevel: Intermediate

Understand the idea

Congratulations! You now understand the foundations of investing. The final step is combining everything you've learned into a simple investment strategy.

Imagine This: Building a house requires bricks, cement, steel, doors and windows. Each material is useful on its own, but only together do they create a home. Investing works the same way.

✅ A simple investing checklist: 1. Understand the business. 2. Check revenue and profit growth. 3. Review debt and financial ratios. 4. Study the price chart. 5. Identify the trend. 6. Diversify your investments. 7. Invest only what you can afford. 8. Stay patient and think long term.
