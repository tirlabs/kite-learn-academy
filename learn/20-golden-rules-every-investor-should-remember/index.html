20 Golden Rules Every Investor Should Remember

3 mins readLevel: All Levels

Understand the idea

The following principles are reminders for making thoughtful investment decisions. They are not shortcuts to guaranteed returns. Use them as a checklist to help you stay informed, patient and aware of risk.

20 principles to remember: 1. Invest only in things you understand. 2. Avoid borrowing money to invest. 3. Diversify rather than depending on one investment. 4. Match investments to your time horizon. 5. Be patient and avoid rushed decisions. 6. Do not react to every market headline. 7. Be cautious of tips and promises of quick profits. 8. Review your portfolio periodically. 9. Keep learning as businesses and markets change. 10. Understand how much you could lose. 11. Avoid panic-driven decisions. 12. Do not let greed replace your plan. 13. Study business quality, not just share-price movement. 14. Read company disclosures and annual reports. 15. Understand costs, taxes and other charges. 16. Respect uncertainty and risk. 17. Invest regularly only when it suits your plan. 18. Check facts before acting on information. 19. Follow a process and review it when evidence changes. 20. Remember that building wealth is a long-term journey, not a race.
