Resistance

3 mins readLevel: Beginner

Understand the idea

Resistance is a price area where a stock has previously faced selling pressure. When the price approaches this area, some investors may choose to sell, making it harder for the price to move higher at that time.

Imagine This: Throw a ball towards a ceiling. The ceiling limits how high the ball can travel. Resistance is a little like that ceiling, but in markets it is an area where selling interest has appeared—not a fixed barrier.

If a stock rises towards the same price area several times and then falls, traders may identify that area as resistance. It can form because investors who bought earlier may want to sell near the price at which they entered, or because other sellers see the price as expensive.
