Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)

3 mins readLevel: Beginner

Understand the idea

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a method of investing a fixed amount at regular intervals, often monthly, into a mutual fund. It is a way to build an investing habit; it is not a separate type of investment and it does not guarantee a profit.

Imagine This: You save a little money every month for a future goal instead of waiting until you have a large amount. An SIP applies a similar regular approach to investing in a mutual fund.

Suppose you invest ₹2,000 every month through an SIP. When the fund's unit price is lower, the same amount buys more units; when it is higher, it buys fewer units. The number of units accumulated depends on the amount invested and the applicable NAV on each instalment date.
