Common Investing Mistakes

2 mins readLevel: Intermediate

Understand the idea

Even experienced investors make mistakes. The goal is not to avoid every mistake, but to avoid the costly ones.

❌ Common mistakes include: • Investing without research • Putting all money into one stock • Following social media tips blindly • Buying because of FOMO • Selling because of panic • Ignoring company fundamentals • Trading without a plan

Imagine This: Driving without knowing your destination usually leads to wrong turns. Investing without a plan often leads to expensive mistakes.
