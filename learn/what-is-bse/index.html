What is BSE?

2 mins readLevel: Beginner

Understand the idea

BSE stands for the BSE Limited, historically known as the Bombay Stock Exchange. It is one of India's principal stock exchanges and provides a regulated marketplace for trading securities listed on it.

What happens on BSE?

Investors place buy and sell orders through brokers. Those orders are routed to the exchange's trading system, where compatible orders may be matched. The exchange operates under market rules and regulatory oversight; clearing and settlement processes then help complete executed trades.

A little history

BSE traces its origins to 1875 and is widely recognised as one of Asia's oldest stock exchanges. Its history is part of the development of India's organised securities market.

Is BSE the same as Sensex?

No. BSE is the exchange. SENSEX is a stock-market index associated with BSE. An index tracks the performance of a selected group of securities according to a defined methodology. It is not the exchange itself.

Can a company be listed on both NSE and BSE?

Some companies have their securities listed on more than one exchange, subject to applicable requirements. This means investors may see the same company's shares quoted on both exchanges. The prices are usually close, but small differences can occur as orders and trades happen.
