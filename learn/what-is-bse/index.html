What is BSE?

2 mins readLevel: Beginner

Understand the idea

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) is one of India’s principal stock exchanges. It provides an organised marketplace where investors can buy and sell securities that are listed on the exchange.

Imagine This: Think of a long-established public marketplace that has served buyers and sellers for generations. The BSE has a similarly long history in India’s financial markets.

The BSE was established in 1875. Today, companies from different industries are listed on it. Investors place orders through registered brokers, and the exchange’s systems match compatible buy and sell orders according to its rules.
