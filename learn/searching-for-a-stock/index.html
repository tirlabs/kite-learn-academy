Searching for a Stock

3 mins readLevel: Beginner

Understand the idea

Every listed company has identifying details, including a name and a trading symbol. A trading symbol, often called a ticker, helps investors find the correct security on an exchange.

Imagine This: You search for a contact on your phone using a name. In a broker’s app, you can search for a company by its name or symbol to find the listed security.

Typing a company name such as Infosys, Reliance Industries or Tata Consultancy Services may show matching results. A search can also return different share classes, derivatives or securities with similar names, depending on the broker app.
