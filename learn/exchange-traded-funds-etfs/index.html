Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs)

3 mins readLevel: Beginner

Understand the idea

An Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) is a fund that holds a collection of investments and whose units are bought and sold on a stock exchange. Like a share, an ETF has a market price that can change during the trading day.

Imagine This: Instead of buying every item in a large shopping basket separately, you buy the basket as one package. An ETF can give you exposure to a group of investments through a single listed unit.

Many ETFs are designed to track an index. For example, a Nifty 50 ETF aims to follow the performance of the Nifty 50, before expenses and tracking differences. It does not guarantee that an investor will earn the same return as the index.
