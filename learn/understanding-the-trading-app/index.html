Understanding the Trading App

3 mins readLevel: Beginner

Understand the idea

Whether you use Zerodha, Groww, Upstox or another broker, most trading apps provide similar basic sections. The names and layout may differ, but the main purpose is to help you find securities, place orders and monitor your account.

Imagine This: Different smartphone brands arrange their settings differently, but most still have contacts, messages and a camera. Trading apps also offer familiar functions in different layouts.

A Watchlist lets you follow selected stocks without owning them. Search helps you find a company. Orders shows instructions you have placed, while Holdings generally shows investments you own for delivery. Positions usually shows open positions, including intraday trades.
