Building Your Personal Investment Plan

3 mins readLevel: Intermediate

Understand the idea

A personal investment plan connects your money decisions to your life goals. It helps you decide what you are investing for, when you may need the money, how much risk you can accept and how you will review your investments.

A simple plan can include: 1. Keep an emergency fund for unexpected expenses. 2. Manage expensive debt and protect essential needs. 3. Write down your financial goals and target dates. 4. Decide how much you can invest without disrupting essential spending. 5. Choose an asset mix that matches your time horizon and risk capacity. 6. Invest through a process you understand. 7. Review your plan periodically and when your circumstances change.

Suppose you are saving for a goal due in two years and another goal due in fifteen years. You may not want to take the same level of market risk with both amounts. The time available and your ability to handle losses should influence the choices.
