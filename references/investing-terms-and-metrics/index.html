Key Investing Terms and Metrics

A guide to common measures investors encounter when researching a listed company.

Market capitalisation

Market capitalisation estimates the market value of a company's listed equity. It is calculated using the share price and outstanding shares; it does not measure intrinsic value.

Earnings per share

EPS expresses the relevant profit attributable to ordinary shareholders on a per-share basis. Check whether figures are basic or diluted and whether they are historical or forecast.

P/E ratio

The P/E ratio compares price with earnings per share. It is meaningful only when earnings are positive and should be interpreted with growth, risk, accounting and sector context.

Return ratios

ROE and ROCE help assess how effectively a business uses equity or capital. Compare companies with similar business models and examine changes over time.

Use metrics as questions

A ratio is a starting point for research, not a verdict. Understand the calculation, the period, the accounting inputs and what may make comparisons misleading.
