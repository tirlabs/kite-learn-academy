Investment Risk and Portfolio Building

Learn how risk, diversification, asset allocation and position sizing fit together.

Risk is more than price movement

Investment risk includes the possibility of loss, uncertain outcomes, liquidity constraints, concentration and the chance that an investment will not meet a financial goal.

Diversification

Holding different investments can reduce the impact of one company or sector on a portfolio. Diversification does not prevent losses when markets fall broadly.

Asset allocation

The mix between shares, bonds, cash and other assets influences portfolio behaviour. It should reflect the goal's time horizon, liquidity needs and ability to tolerate losses.

Position sizing

Position sizing determines how much capital is exposed to one investment. A sensible position size limits the damage a single wrong decision can cause.

Review the whole portfolio

Look at concentration, overlap, costs, liquidity and whether the portfolio still matches the goal. Avoid changing a plan solely because of short-term market noise.

Educational information only. This guide is for understanding concepts, not a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Market rules, charges and tax treatment can change; verify current information with official sources and your broker.
