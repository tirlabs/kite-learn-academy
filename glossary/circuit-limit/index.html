Upper and Lower Circuit

Meaning

Exchange-defined price limits that restrict how far a security can move during a trading session. Applicable limits and mechanisms vary by security and exchange rules.

Why it matters

Price limits can affect whether orders are executed during sharp price movements. Understanding them helps explain why a security may reach a permitted limit while buy or sell orders remain unmatched.

Simple example

If a security reaches its applicable lower price limit, additional sell orders may not execute until eligible buying interest appears or market conditions change.

A definition is a starting point, not an investment recommendation. Check current rules and product documents before making financial decisions.
