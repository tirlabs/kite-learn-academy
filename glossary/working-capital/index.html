Working Capital

Meaning

A measure commonly calculated as current assets minus current liabilities. It offers a snapshot of short-term operating resources, but its meaning depends on the business model.

Why it matters

Working capital gives a snapshot of short-term operating resources after current liabilities are considered. Its meaning varies by business: retailers, manufacturers and service companies can have very different operating cycles.

Simple example

If a company has current assets of ₹80 lakh and current liabilities of ₹55 lakh, its simplified working capital is ₹25 lakh. The composition and timing of those balances still need to be examined.

A definition is a starting point, not an investment recommendation. Check current rules and product documents before making financial decisions.
