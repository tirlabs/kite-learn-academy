Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio

Meaning

A valuation ratio comparing a share's market price with earnings per share. A higher or lower P/E is not automatically good or bad; context matters.

A definition is a starting point, not an investment recommendation. Check current rules and product documents before making financial decisions.
