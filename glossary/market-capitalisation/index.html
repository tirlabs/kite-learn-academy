Market Capitalisation

Meaning

The market value of a company's listed equity, commonly calculated as share price multiplied by outstanding shares. It measures size, not business quality or valuation.

Why it matters

Market capitalisation is a measure of the market value of listed equity, often used to group companies by size. It does not tell you whether the business is profitable, financially strong or undervalued.

Simple example

If a company has 10 crore outstanding shares and each share trades at ₹50, its illustrative market capitalisation is ₹500 crore.

A definition is a starting point, not an investment recommendation. Check current rules and product documents before making financial decisions.
