Capital Gains

Meaning

Profit arising when an asset is sold for more than its purchase cost, subject to applicable tax rules. Tax treatment depends on the asset, holding period and current law.

Why it matters

Capital gains are relevant when an asset is sold or otherwise transferred. The taxable amount and applicable rate depend on current law, asset type, holding period and other details; a simple price difference may not equal the final taxable gain.

Simple example

If an investment is bought for ₹5,000 and later sold for ₹6,200, the basic difference is ₹1,200 before considering eligible costs, exemptions or applicable tax rules.

A definition is a starting point, not an investment recommendation. Check current rules and product documents before making financial decisions.
