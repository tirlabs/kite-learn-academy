Diversification

Meaning

Spreading investments across different securities, sectors or asset classes so one holding has less influence on the overall portfolio. It can reduce concentration risk but cannot eliminate market risk.

Why it matters

Diversification reduces dependence on a single holding or source of return. It cannot remove market-wide risk, and holdings that look different may still move together.

Simple example

A portfolio holding shares from only one company is exposed heavily to that company. Spreading exposure across different companies and asset classes can reduce the effect of one company's problem.

A definition is a starting point, not an investment recommendation. Check current rules and product documents before making financial decisions.
