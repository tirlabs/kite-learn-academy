Secondary Market

Meaning

The market where existing securities are bought and sold between investors, typically through an exchange or another permitted trading venue.

Why it matters

The secondary market provides a venue for investors to trade existing securities. Trading activity can support liquidity and price discovery, but a buyer's payment generally goes to the selling investor rather than directly to the company.

Simple example

After a company's IPO shares are listed, one investor may sell shares to another investor through an exchange. That transaction takes place in the secondary market.

A definition is a starting point, not an investment recommendation. Check current rules and product documents before making financial decisions.
