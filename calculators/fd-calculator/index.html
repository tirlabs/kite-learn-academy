A fixed deposit places a lump sum with a bank or financial institution for a chosen tenure and interest convention. This estimate shows the effect of a fixed rate and selected compounding frequency.

How is this calculated?

Estimate a fixed deposit maturity value. The calculation uses the following method:

Maturity value = P × (1 + r ÷ n)n × t

Worked example

A ₹1,00,000 deposit at an assumed 7% annual rate for 3 years, compounded quarterly, gives an illustrative maturity value of about ₹1,23,144.

Assumptions and limitations

Actual FD products may use specific day-count rules, payout options, senior-citizen rates, premature-withdrawal penalties and tax treatment. Confirm the institution’s terms before relying on a figure.

Use the inputs as an illustration, not as a prediction or recommendation. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to KiteLearn.
