Worked example

For a ₹5,000 monthly deposit over 5 years, the total amount contributed is ₹3,00,000. The estimated maturity value adds interest based on the chosen rate and the model’s month-end instalment timing. Each deposit begins earning interest in the following month in this illustration.

Assumptions and limitations

This educational model approximates quarterly compounding using an equivalent monthly rate and month-end deposits. Banks can use product-specific conventions and rounding; check the bank’s own maturity quote.

Use the inputs as an illustration, not as a prediction or recommendation. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to KiteLearn.
