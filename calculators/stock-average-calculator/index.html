When you buy more shares at a different price, your average purchase price changes. The correct average is weighted by the number of shares in each purchase, not the simple average of the two prices.

How is this calculated?

Calculate the weighted average purchase price. The calculation uses the following method:

Weighted average = (Quantity 1 × Price 1 + Quantity 2 × Price 2) ÷ Total quantity

Worked example

Buying 10 shares at ₹250 and another 10 at ₹230 gives 20 shares at a weighted average of ₹240 each, before charges.

Assumptions and limitations

This calculation combines two purchase lots and excludes brokerage, taxes, corporate actions and any shares sold between purchases. Add all relevant lots for a complete cost basis.
