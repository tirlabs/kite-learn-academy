An Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) combines principal repayment and interest. In a standard reducing-balance loan, the interest portion is higher at the beginning and the principal portion generally rises over time.

How is this calculated?

Understand the cost of borrowing. The calculation uses the following method:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1], where r is monthly interest and n is the number of instalments.

Worked example

For a ₹25,00,000 loan at 9% annual interest over 240 months, the calculator estimates the monthly EMI and separates total repayment into principal and interest.

Assumptions and limitations

Assumes a fixed annual rate converted to a monthly rate and equal monthly instalments. It excludes processing fees, insurance, rate resets, prepayments and lender-specific rounding. At 0% interest, EMI is principal divided by instalments.

Use the inputs as an illustration, not as a prediction or recommendation. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to KiteLearn.
