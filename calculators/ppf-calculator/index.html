Understand your result

Change the inputs to explore how the result changes.

The Public Provident Fund is a long-term government-backed savings scheme with prescribed contribution limits, tenure and interest rules. This calculator is an illustration using an editable rate. It assumes each annual contribution is made by 5 April, applies one-twelfth of the entered annual rate to the eligible balance each month, and credits the accumulated interest annually. The entered rate is held constant, although the notified PPF rate can change over time.

How is this calculated?

Explore long-term savings with PPF assumptions. The calculation uses the following method:

Each year: add the contribution by 5 April; calculate monthly interest on the eligible balance; credit the year’s interest

Worked example

With a ₹1,50,000 contribution made by 5 April each year and a constant assumed 7.1% rate for 15 years, the model estimates the future balance. Actual PPF interest depends on the eligible monthly balance and government-notified rates; this is not an official PPF statement.

Assumptions and limitations

The model assumes contributions at the beginning of each year and annual compounding. Actual PPF interest is calculated using scheme rules, including monthly balance timing, and rates may be revised. It does not calculate tax benefits or eligibility.

Use the inputs as an illustration, not as a prediction or recommendation. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to KiteLearn.
