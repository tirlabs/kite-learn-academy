How is this calculated?

See how interest can earn interest. The calculation uses the following method:

A = P × (1 + r ÷ n)n × t

Worked example

A ₹1,00,000 deposit at an assumed 8% annual rate, compounded quarterly for 10 years, grows to approximately ₹2,20,804 before taxes and charges.

Assumptions and limitations

This model assumes a fixed rate and equal compounding periods. It does not model changing rates, deposits, withdrawals, tax or inflation.

Use the inputs as an illustration, not as a prediction or recommendation. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to KiteLearn.
