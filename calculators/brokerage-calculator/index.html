Understand your result

A trade’s gross price difference is not the same as its net result. Brokerage and other transaction charges reduce the amount you keep. Enter your own charge assumptions because broker plans and statutory rates differ.

How is this calculated?

Estimate trading charges and net trade outcome. The calculation uses the following method:

Net trade result = (Sell price − Buy price) × quantity − brokerage − other entered charges

Worked example

If 100 shares are bought at ₹100 and sold at ₹110, the gross price difference is ₹1,000. Subtract the brokerage entered for both sides and the other charges to estimate the net result.

Assumptions and limitations

This calculator uses user-entered brokerage and a single combined other-charge amount. It does not calculate exchange-specific statutory levies, STT, GST, stamp duty or DP charges separately. Verify the current contract note for exact charges.

Use the inputs as an illustration, not as a prediction or recommendation. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to KiteLearn.
