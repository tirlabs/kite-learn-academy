Dividend income depends on the number of shares held and the dividend declared per share. Indicated dividend yield compares the dividend per share with the market price entered.

How is this calculated?

Estimate dividend income and indicated yield. The calculation uses the following method:

Dividend income = Shares × Dividend per share; indicated yield = Dividend per share ÷ Market price × 100.

Worked example

For 100 shares and a dividend of ₹5 per share, gross dividend income is ₹500. At a market price of ₹250 per share, the indicated yield is 2%.

Assumptions and limitations

Dividend declarations can change and are not guaranteed. The yield is based only on the entered dividend and market price; it excludes tax, timing, eligibility dates and reinvestment.

Use the inputs as an illustration, not as a prediction or recommendation. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to KiteLearn.
