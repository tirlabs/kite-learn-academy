Understand your result

Inflation reduces purchasing power: the same amount of money may buy fewer goods and services in the future. This calculator projects a future cost using one constant assumed inflation rate.

How is this calculated?

Understand how prices may change over time. The calculation uses the following method:

Future cost = Current cost × (1 + inflation rate)years

Worked example

If an expense costs ₹10,000 today and inflation averages an assumed 6% for 10 years, its future equivalent cost is about ₹17,908.

Assumptions and limitations

Inflation differs across goods, households and time. This is a single-rate illustration, not an official inflation forecast or a personal cost-of-living index.

Use the inputs as an illustration, not as a prediction or recommendation. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to KiteLearn.
