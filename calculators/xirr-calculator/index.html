Understand your result

XIRR estimates the annualised money-weighted return for a series of dated investments and receipts. It is useful when contributions or withdrawals happen on irregular dates. Enter one cash flow per line as date,amount. Use negative amounts for money invested and positive amounts for money received.

How is this calculated?

Measure returns when cash flows happen on different dates. The calculation uses the following method:

Find r such that Σ [cash flow ÷ (1 + r)(days from first date ÷ 365)] = 0

Worked example

Example: enter an investment of −100000 on 2024-01-01 and a receipt of 120000 on 2025-01-01. The result is close to 20% annualised, subject to the exact day count.

Assumptions and limitations

A valid solution may not exist, or multiple solutions may exist for unusual cash-flow patterns. This calculator searches for one solution in a bounded rate range. Check dates, signs and cash-flow completeness.

Use the inputs as an illustration, not as a prediction or recommendation. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to KiteLearn.
