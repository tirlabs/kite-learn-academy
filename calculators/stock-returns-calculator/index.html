Understand your result

Change the inputs to explore how the result changes.

A stock investment result should consider purchase cost, sale proceeds, charges and any dividends received. This calculator combines the amounts you enter to estimate a net rupee profit or loss.

How is this calculated?

See the effect of charges and dividends on stock returns. The calculation uses the following method:

Net result = Sale proceeds − purchase cost − entered charges + dividends received.

Worked example

If 100 shares are bought at ₹100 and sold at ₹125, the gross price gain is ₹2,500. Enter purchase and selling charges and dividends to see the adjusted result.

Assumptions and limitations

This is a simple single-purchase, single-sale model. It does not annualise returns or calculate statutory charges automatically. It excludes partial sales, corporate actions and tax.

Use the inputs as an illustration, not as a prediction or recommendation. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to KiteLearn.
