Total amount invested

Estimated growth

How is the SIP estimate calculated?

The calculator uses the future-value formula for a series of equal monthly contributions (an ordinary annuity):

FV = P × (((1 + r)n − 1) ÷ r)

P is the monthly investment, r is the assumed monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12), and n is the total number of monthly contributions (years × 12). If the assumed return is 0%, the future value is simply the total amount invested.

Timing assumption: each monthly contribution is assumed to be invested at the end of the month. The monthly rate is calculated as the annual assumed rate divided by 12, with monthly compounding.

What this estimate does not include

  • Market volatility or changes in actual mutual fund returns.
  • Expense ratios, taxes, exit loads or other charges.
  • The effect of inflation on future purchasing power.
  • Missed, delayed or changing monthly contributions.

Frequently asked questions

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a way to invest a fixed amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals, commonly every month. The calculator estimates the possible future value of those contributions using an assumed rate of return.

How does this SIP calculator work?

It adds the future value of each monthly contribution using a monthly rate derived from the annual assumed return. This version assumes each contribution is made at the end of the month and compounds monthly.

Are SIP calculator returns guaranteed?

No. The assumed annual return is only an input for illustration. Mutual fund returns vary with market conditions, and the actual value may be higher or lower, including a loss of capital.

Does this calculator include taxes, fees or inflation?

No. The estimate does not deduct taxes, expense ratios, exit loads or other costs, and it does not adjust the result for inflation. These factors affect the purchasing power and amount you may receive.
