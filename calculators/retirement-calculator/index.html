Retirement planning starts by estimating how today’s living expenses may change with inflation, then considering how a corpus could support withdrawals during retirement. This tool estimates a target corpus under simplified assumptions.

How is this calculated?

Estimate the corpus needed for future expenses. The calculation uses the following method:

Future monthly expense = Current expense × (1 + inflation)years to retirement; corpus is the present value of assumed retirement expenses.

Worked example

If monthly expenses are ₹50,000 today, inflation of 6% over 25 years would make the equivalent monthly expense about ₹2.15 lakh at retirement. The corpus estimate then depends on the assumed post-retirement return and retirement length.

Assumptions and limitations

This is a simplified planning illustration. It assumes a constant inflation rate and return, level real spending pattern and no other income, taxes, healthcare shocks or legacy target. It does not determine how much you must invest today.

Use the inputs as an illustration, not as a prediction or recommendation. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to KiteLearn.
