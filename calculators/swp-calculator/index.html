Understand your result

Change the inputs to explore how the result changes.

A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) models regular withdrawals from an existing corpus. The remaining balance depends on withdrawal size, timing and the return assumption.

How is this calculated?

Explore a planned withdrawal from a portfolio. The calculation uses the following method:

Balance each month = Previous balance × (1 + monthly rate) − withdrawal

Worked example

A ₹10,00,000 corpus with ₹10,000 withdrawn each month is exposed to both withdrawals and the assumed monthly growth. Changing either input can materially change how long the corpus lasts.

Assumptions and limitations

This simplified projection applies assumed growth before each month-end withdrawal. Actual investment returns are volatile, withdrawals may occur on different dates, and taxes or fees are excluded. A zero balance means the modelled corpus is exhausted.

Use the inputs as an illustration, not as a prediction or recommendation. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to KiteLearn.
